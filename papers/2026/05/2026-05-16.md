## Top Papers

### 1. Hand-in-the-Loop: Improving Dexterous VLA via Seamless Interventional Correction
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.15157
- **Authors:** Zhuohang Li, Liqun Huang, Wei Xu, Zhengming Zhu, Nie Lin, Xiao Ma, Xinjun Sheng, Ruoshi Wen
- **Summary:** Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are prone to compounding errors in dexterous manipulation, where high-dimensional action spaces and contact-rich dynamics amplify small policy deviations over long horizons. We present Hand-in-the-Loop (HandITL), a seamless human-in-the-loop intervention method that blends human corrective intent with autonomous policy execution to avoid gesture jumps during bimanual dexterous manipulation. HandITL reduces takeover jitter by 99.8% and grasp failures by 87.5%.
- **中文摘要：** VLA模型在灵巧操作中容易出现复合误差。本文提出HandITL，一种无缝人机协同干预方法，将人类纠正意图与自主策略执行混合，避免双手灵巧操作中的手势跳跃。相比直接遥操作接管，接管抖动降低99.8%，抓取失败率降低87.5%，平均完成时间减少19.1%。

### 2. Pelican-Unified 1.0: A Unified Embodied Intelligence Model for Understanding, Reasoning, Imagination and Action
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.15153
- **Authors:** Yi Zhang, Yinda Chen, Che Liu, Zeyuan Ding, Jin Xu, Shilong Zou, Junwei Liao, Jiayu Hu, Xiancong Ren, Xiaopeng Zhang, Yechi Liu, Haoyuan Shi, Zecong Tang, Haosong Sun, Renwen Cui, Kuishu Wu, Wenhai Liu, Yang Xu, Yingji Zhang, Yidong Wang, Senkang Hu, Jinpeng Lu, Nga Teng Chan, Yechen Wu, Yong Dai, Jian Tang, Xiaozhu Ju
- **Summary:** We present Pelican-Unified 1.0, the first embodied foundation model trained according to the principle of unification. It uses a single VLM as a unified understanding and reasoning module, and a Unified Future Generator (UFG) that jointly generates future videos and future actions. Achieves 64.7 on eight VLM benchmarks, 66.03 on WorldArena (ranking first), and 93.5 on RoboTwin.
- **中文摘要：** 本文提出Pelican-Unified 1.0，首个按照统一原则训练的具身基础模型。使用单一VLM作为统一的理解和推理模块，以及统一未来生成器(UFG)联合生成未来视频和动作。在8个VLM基准上达到64.7分，WorldArena排名第一（66.03分），RoboTwin上达到93.5分。

### 3. CLOVER: Closed-Loop Value Estimation & Ranking for End-to-End Autonomous Driving Planning
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.15120
- **Authors:** Sining Ang, Yuguang Yang, Canyu Chen, Yan Wang
- **Summary:** We propose CLOVER, a Closed-LOop Value Estimation and Ranking framework for end-to-end autonomous driving planning. A generator produces diverse candidate trajectories, and a scorer predicts planning-metric sub-scores to rank them. On NAVSIM, CLOVER achieves 94.5 PDMS and 90.4 EPDMS, establishing a new state of the art.
- **中文摘要：** 本文提出CLOVER，一种端到端自动驾驶规划的闭环价值估计与排序框架。生成器产生多样化候选轨迹，评分器预测规划指标子分数进行排序。在NAVSIM上达到94.5 PDMS和90.4 EPDMS，建立新的最先进水平。
